# Project context

- **What:** a Sem VI Computer Engineering mini project at VCET (University of Mumbai), A.Y. 2025-26. "BRIDGIFY: Bridging Communication Gaps." `[CONFIRMED report cover]`
- **Team:** Riddhi Mehta (03), Vedika Misal (04), Siddhi Bhosale (20), Om Panjari (02). **Mentor:** Prof. Manali Payghan. HoD: Dr. Megha Trivedi. `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Audience for v2:**
  - the mentor and examiners, who need a working live link and the codebase
  - a possible patent filing
  - real users, people with communication disabilities and their caregivers
- **Money:** none. The product is free to users (report aim), and the project must run on free services (D-18).
- **What must never happen:**
  - a camera or mic stream leaves the device without the user knowing
  - a feature that the report claims is faked
  - an unlicensed sign video is shipped
  - the patent idea is disclosed publicly before filing
- **History:**
  - v1 was built on Lovable ("Bridgefy Connect"). The report's screenshots come from an earlier build.
  - v1's sign detection was simulated and its text→sign used emoji.
  - v2 is a ground-up rebuild.
